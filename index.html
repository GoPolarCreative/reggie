Our Services

What We Offer

Comprehensive NDIS supports designed to help participants live independently, engage with their community, and achieve their goals.

Home Care

Personal support delivered in the comfort of your own home — tailored to your routine, preferences, and goals.

Supported Independent Living (SIL)

Shared living arrangements with 24/7 support across Melbourne's Northern and Western suburbs.

NDIS Housing & Tenancy Support

Helping participants find, transition into, and maintain suitable, stable housing that supports their independence.

Short & Medium Term Accommodation

Flexible respite and transitional accommodation options — STA and MTA — for when you need a change or break.

Community Access & Social Engagement

Get out, connect, and participate in community life with our dedicated support workers by your side.

Assist Personal Activities

Daily personal care — from hygiene and grooming to meal preparation — tailored precisely to your needs and NDIS goals.

Development of Life Skills

Building lasting independence through practical skill development — from budgeting and cooking to communication and self-advocacy.

Group & Centre Activities

Engaging group programs and community-based activities that foster connection, skill-building, and fun.

Our Story

About Reggie Care Services

At Reggie Care Services, we believe every person with a disability deserves to live life on their own terms. Founded by veterans and nurses, we bring discipline, clinical expertise, and genuine heart to everything we do.

Our mission is to enhance the lives of participants and their families through exceptional care, empowering personal choice, and creating opportunities for positive, lasting outcomes.

We serve the Northern and Western suburbs of Melbourne, and we're proud to be a community-built, community-focused provider.
